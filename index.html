Ketharnath

Video Editor & Visual Communicator

I started my journey with creative design and visual arts, exploring visual layouts and creative storytelling ideas. Over the past years, I've worked across video editing, compositing, and modern digital interfaces, gradually understanding how everything connects and works together.

With time, my focus shifted from just making things look attractive to communicating ideas clearly. That's when I began to see myself more as a brand visualizer and communicator, not just a standard editor.

Now, I integrate web design and interactive visuals to understand how modern experiences work in practice. It's helping me think beyond static graphics and focus deeply on how people interact, experience, and engage with content.

My goal is to continue evolving by blending cinematic video production with interactive web experiences to craft work that feels clear, useful, and meaningful.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

Quick answers before you reach out — anything else, just ask.

What's your typical turnaround time?
Most single video edits ship within 3–5 business days. Larger campaigns, full brand packages or web builds get a clear timeline on the discovery call once scope is set.
Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes — everything is remote-friendly. Calls are scheduled around your timezone, and files move through shared drives or direct transfer, whichever you prefer.
What do I actually receive at the end?
Export-ready files in the formats you need — MP4/MOV masters for video, layered PSDs or flattened exports for stills, and a deployed or handed-off codebase for web projects.
How does pricing and payment work?
Every project is scoped and quoted individually after the discovery call, so pricing reflects the actual work involved rather than a flat rate. A deposit secures the start date, with the balance due on delivery.
